# Sovereign Power Systems - Industrial Steam Turbine Manual
Model Series: TG-8500
Revision: 3.2 (Controlled Industrial Release)

## Section 1: Core Operational Limits
The TG-8500 industrial steam turbine is designed for continuous baseload power generation.
- Rated Nominal Capacity: 1250 kW
- Operating Speed: 3600 RPM
- Over-speed Trip Threshold: 3960 RPM (110% of rated speed)
- Maximum Allowable Bearing Vibration: 4.5 mm/s RMS under steady load.
- Critical Vibration Trip Limit: 7.0 mm/s RMS (immediate automated trip).
- Maximum Main Bearing Temperature: 95.0 deg C.
- Normal Oil Header Pressure: 2.2 to 2.8 bar.

## Section 2: Lubrication and Oil Circuit
- Specified Lubricant: ISO VG 46 synthetic rust-and-oxidation inhibited turbine oil.
- Oil Reservoir Total Capacity: 800 Liters.
- Lube Oil Change Interval: Every 8000 operating hours or 12 calendar months, whichever occurs first.
- Lube Oil Filter Differential Pressure Alarm: 1.2 bar.
- Minimum Oil Supply Temperature to Bearings: 40.0 deg C prior to rolling turbine.

## Section 3: Fasteners and Mechanical Specifications
- Turbine Casing Main Split-Flange Studs: M36 grade 8.8 alloy steel.
- Specified Casing Stud Tightening Torque: 450 Nm (tightened in cross-pattern sequence).
- Steam Admission Stop Valve Flange Bolts: 180 Nm torque.
- Exhaust Vacuum Normal Operating Range: 0.06 bar to 0.10 bar absolute.
- Gland Steam Sealing Pressure: 0.25 bar gauge.

## Section 4: Emergency Trip & Interlock Sequences
1. Manual Emergency Trip: Hand trip push-button PB-101 located on front governor pedestal.
2. Low Lube Oil Pressure Trip: Activated if header pressure drops below 1.2 bar for more than 500 ms.
3. High Exhaust Temperature Trip: Activated if exhaust hood temperature exceeds 120.0 deg C.
