# Industrial Fluid Handling - Boiler Feedwater Pump Maintenance Standard
Document Number: SPEC-PUMP-402
Revision: 1.4

## Section 1: Boiler Feedwater Pumps P-101 and P-102
Feedwater pumps P-101 (Primary) and P-102 (Auxiliary Standby) are multistage centrifugal pumps.
- Design Operating Discharge Pressure: 180.0 psi nominal (Maximum rated: 220.0 psi).
- Nominal Volumetric Flow Rate: 350 GPM at 3550 RPM.
- Impeller Diameter: 245 mm stainless steel grade 316.
- Minimum Continuous Stable Flow (MCSF): 80 GPM.
- Mechanical Seal Type: Balanced cartridge seal with API Plan 11 flush arrangement.
- Seal Flush Minimum Recommended Flow: 15 GPM from discharge recirculation orifice.

## Section 2: Preventive Maintenance Procedures
- Daily Inspection: Check seal flush temperature (must stay below 60 deg C) and bearing housing vibration.
- Monthly Inspection: Verify coupling alignment and grease cavity level on electric drive motor.
- Annual Overhaul: Disassemble casing wear rings; replace if diametrical clearance exceeds 0.45 mm.
- Coupling Spacer Bolt Torque: 85 Nm.
