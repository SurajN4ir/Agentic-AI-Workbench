# Facility Administration - Administrative Guidelines & Employee Services
Memo ID: MEMO-ADM-2026-09
Department: Corporate Operations

## Section 1: Cafeteria Operating Schedule
The central employee cafeteria located in Building 4 provides meal services according to the following schedule:
- Breakfast Hours: 07:00 AM to 09:30 AM
- Lunch Service: 11:30 AM to 02:00 PM
- Evening Refreshments: 04:30 PM to 06:30 PM
- Vending machines and coffee stations are accessible 24/7 with badge access.

## Section 2: Parking Allocations & Badge Permits
- Parking Lot A is reserved exclusively for marked maintenance utility vans and heavy delivery carriers.
- Parking Lot B is open for general employee passenger vehicles displaying a valid Green permit decal.
- Electric vehicle charging stalls require pre-registration through the facility intranet portal.
- Speed limit on all plant roadways is strictly enforced at 15 km/h.
